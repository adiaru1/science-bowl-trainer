Controls & Rules

  • Voice Answer: After buzzing, press V or click Speak to dictate your response.
  • Typing Guard: While actively typing after a buzz, the 2.5-second stall clock pauses automatically.
  • Saved Cards: Click Save Card on any question to store it in your review deck.
  • Weakest Category: Select Weakest Category in the filter bar to drill your lowest-scoring subject.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Press Space to buzz, V to speak, B for bonus, and N for next question.
